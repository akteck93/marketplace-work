HOW IT WORKS

One marketplace. Two focused workflows.

01

Post or Discover

Clients publish requirements. Providers search relevant projects and opportunities.

02

Compare & Select

Providers submit proposals. Clients shortlist, reject or convert the best proposal into an offer.

03

Deliver & Review

Accepted offers become contracts with milestones, workroom communication and feedback.
